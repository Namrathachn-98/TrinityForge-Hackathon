## 📌 About the Project

AdhereX AI is a predictive healthcare platform concept designed to help address long-term treatment non-adherence among patients with chronic diseases.

The project focuses on identifying behavioral changes that may indicate a risk of treatment abandonment. By combining predictive analytics, emotion-aware AI interactions, and 3D health visualization, the platform aims to support patients and help caregivers identify when additional attention may be needed.

## 🎯 Problem Statement

Maintaining consistent treatment over long periods can be challenging for people living with chronic diseases. Behavioral, emotional, and lifestyle changes may affect medication adherence and treatment continuity.

AdhereX AI explores how intelligent monitoring and timely interventions could help identify potential risks earlier.

## ✨ Key Features

- **Predictive Behavioral Drift Detection** — identifies patterns that may indicate changes in treatment adherence.
- **14-Day Risk Forecasting** — explores forecasting the risk of treatment abandonment.
- **Emotion-Aware AI Support** — designed to provide more personalized patient interactions.
- **3D Digital Twin Visualization** — visualizes anatomical models to support an interactive healthcare experience.
- **Adaptive Reminders** — supports timely treatment reminders.
- **Caregiver Escalation** — aims to help caregivers respond when additional support may be needed.
- **Health Stability Dashboard** — provides a visual overview of relevant health and behavioral indicators.
